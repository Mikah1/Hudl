The big five · constant acceleration

vf = v0 + at
Δs = ½(v0 + vf)t
Δs = v0t + ½at²
Δs = vft − ½at²
vf² = v0² + 2aΔs

Apply each equation to one axis at a time. Resolve the launch: v₀ₓ = v₀ cos θ, v₀ᵧ = v₀ sin θ. Here +y is upward, aᵧ = −9.81 m/s², and aₓ = 0. Average velocity is Δs/t.
